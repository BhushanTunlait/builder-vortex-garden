Privacy Policy

AI Workers · last updated 29 October 2026

AI Workers is a personal tool run by Bhushan Tunlait on his own computer. It helps prepare and upload short videos to his own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, AI Workers asks for permission to:

  • upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and set their title, description, tags and settings, and
  • read basic information and view counts for the owner's own channel and videos.

It does not read email, contacts, files, or any other Google data, and it does not access other people's accounts.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to show the owner how they perform.
  • Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and are not used for advertising.
  • Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's data folder removes everything it stored.
